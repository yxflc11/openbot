title: "Contribute a capability"
description: "Contribute a capability — OpenBot"
---

Anyone can build a compatible MCP service. To propose it for the reviewed OpenBot catalog, open the [plugin submission form](https://github.com/yxflc11/openbot/issues/new?template=plugin-submission.yml).

Include a public source repository, exact release or commit, an open-source license, setup instructions, authentication model, declared tools/resources/prompts/views and all external effects. Describe supported platforms and supply reproducible success and failure tests. Do not include a real token, account credential or private transcript.

A maintainer reviews protocol fit, permissions, dependency provenance, maintenance, usability and test evidence. A catalog entry must point to the reviewed revision and make operational requirements clear. Approval of a listing does not auto-install it for existing users; every Owner still previews and grants access.

For a capability that belongs in the core product, discuss its scope before expanding the Server or adding a dependency. Prefer an open standard or maintained package, record the research and license decision, keep authorization in the Server, and include English canonical documentation with a Chinese translation. Read [CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/yxflc11/openbot/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [repository map](https://github.com/yxflc11/openbot/blob/main/docs/REPOSITORY_MAP.md).
