## Retained configuration and cleanup

At the user's request, the local, Git-ignored `.env` retains `MOONSHOT_API_KEY`,
`MOONSHOT_BASE_URL=https://api.moonshot.cn/v1`, and `MOONSHOT_MODEL=kimi-k3`. Its permissions are
`0600`; the key is not reproduced here. The latter settings record the intended future connection;
the current OpenBot runtime does not consume them. Test services and the disposable database were
removed. Temporary probes and logs were deleted after recording these results.
